Weather & what to bring

🌦️ British summer is famously unpredictable — expect warm(ish) days and cooler evenings, and pack for both sunshine and a passing shower.
👟 The barn sits in open fields, so heels sink into grass. Flats, wedges or block heels are your friend outdoors.
🧥 Bring a layer for once the sun goes down — evenings can turn chilly even in August.
☂️ A small umbrella never hurts. This is England, after all.

Good to know

Accommodation

There are a number of places to stay nearby:

Quy Mill Hotel — premium option, a short drive from the venue.

Novotel Cambridge North — by Cambridge North station, where some of the family are staying.

Cambridge Travelodge — affordable option with many other guests staying.

Lodeside Glamping — the nearest affordable option, walkable from the venue.

There are also plenty of Airbnbs around Lode, Reach, and Swaffham Bulbeck.

Travel

The venue is accessible by taxi from both Cambridge station and Cambridge North station.

Parking

Free on-site parking at the barn. Cars can be left overnight.
