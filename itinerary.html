What to bring or wear

👟 The ground is grass and can be soft, so flat shoes or wedges/block heels are easiest.
🧥 It might get cold in the evening, so bring a layer.

Getting there

🚕 There's no public transport from central Cambridge to the barn, so a taxi is the easiest way. It's about a 20 minute drive from either Cambridge or Cambridge North station.
🚌 We're arranging shuttle buses from the stations and the Cambridge Travelodge. More details to follow.
🅿️ Free parking at the barn. You can leave your car overnight.

Gifts

More than anything, we'd love you to give to CHINAR International.

CHINAR (Child Nurture and Relief) works with orphans, widows and young people affected by conflict in Jammu and Kashmir. They run community learning centres across the region, supporting education, nutrition, health and skills training.

You can give through the Donate button on their website.
